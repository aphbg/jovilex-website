The Campus
Thirteen buildings. One standard.

Each building serves a distinct function. The campus is zoned by security clearance: a high-security cluster (Trading Floor, GovTech Wing, Data Centre — biometric entry, restricted access) and a public-access cluster (Operations, Hub, Kitchen, Exhale — open to the campus and the public). All buildings are interconnected by walkways and green corridors. The entire campus is powered by a dedicated solar farm with lithium-grade battery storage — zero national grid dependency, zero diesel generators. The campus generates and stores all its own power permanently.

Operating Principles
24-hour shift architecture
Three or four humans per role on 6-8 hour shifts. Start times rotate — 8am some weeks, 10am others, 4pm others. No one resumes at 9am for their entire career. Two-hour overlap between every shift change: the incoming person has an arrival and centering session, the outgoing person enters The Exhale for decompression. The campus never closes because the humans are always fresh. No one is grinding — the system rotates.
Sound architecture
Every building on campus is intentionally designed with a sonic environment that enhances life and work. Alpha and focus-wave frequencies during work hours. Theta waves for creativity in the afternoon. Ambient wind-down frequencies in the evening. Recovery frequencies in the Wellness Centre. Natural sound in the corridors. The campus has a sound architect the way it has a structural engineer. Sound is infrastructure, not ambiance.
Recovery is infrastructure
Sleep, movement, decompression, and access to nature are not benefits. They are operational requirements engineered into every building and every walkway on the campus.
Elruta feeds everything
Every meal, snack, and drink consumed on campus comes through Elruta. One nutrition standard across every building. What enters the body is designed, not chosen from a menu. The Kitchen, The Exhale, The Forum, the Wellness Centre — all supplied by the same system.
Parents don't choose
Childcare and education are on campus. No team member chooses between their work and their family. The child learns at Praxis. The parent builds at Jovilex. They are 200 metres apart.
Green is structural
Plants, herbs, grass, and natural light are not decoration. They are design requirements in every building and every outdoor space. People put their feet on grass during their workday.
One standard, applied globally
What is built in Abuja defines the DNA for every Komir workspace worldwide. This campus is the reference point. Every future location meets this standard or does not carry the name.
