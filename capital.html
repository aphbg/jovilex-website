KOMIR CAPITAL BUILDING

Not a trading floor — a purpose-built facility designed around one principle: the machine trades, the human governs. Agents run first. Humans enter when scale demands oversight the machine cannot provide. Designed for a decade of operation, institutional-grade security, and a culture that protects the people inside it.
THE INDEPENDENCE RULE — PERFORMANCE PSYCHOLOGY
The Performance Psychologist does not report to the Director of Capital on individual operator state. The Psychologist can pull any operator off the desk — "you are not fit for shift" — and no Desk Lead, Zone Lead, or Director can override that decision. This is the flight-surgeon model: the person responsible for your mind cannot be the person responsible for your P&L. The Director sees aggregate wellness metrics (shift absences, rotation requests, burnout flags) but never individual session content. The operators must trust that what they say in the room stays in the room — or the room is useless.

Reporting line: Performance Psychologist → Komir Holdings HR (not Capital & Treasury). Authority: fitness-for-duty rulings are final for 24 hours. Appeal goes to HR, not the trading desk.

MEZZANINE
THE BRIDGE — CENTRAL COMMAND
Elevated. Overlooking the hall. The system-level view
COMMAND WALL
6-panel video wall. Aggregate equity curves across all 20 desks. Gate status lights (green/amber/red per desk). Total AUM. Daily P&L. Cross-desk correlation alerts. News feed with blackout countdown.
RISK STATION
Head of Risk sits here — independent from desk leads. Monitors aggregate exposure. Can freeze any desk instantly. Cross-desk correlation is the primary danger: if 12 desks are long gold simultaneously, that's not 12 independent positions — it's one massive bet.
DIRECTOR'S STATION
Director of Capital & Treasury. Capital allocation decisions. New desk approvals. Weekly performance reviews. The only person who can override the Head of Risk — and only in writing, with a logged justification.
FLOOR 3
THE HUMAN WING
The machine does not need this floor. The people do
PERFORMANCE PSYCHOLOGY SUITE
Two private consultation rooms. Soundproofed. No cameras. The Performance Psychologist conducts regular check-ins (weekly for active operators, post-incident for anyone involved in a gate fire). Independent reporting line — HR, not Capital.
The operator who watched Gate 1 fire and the machine stop itself needs to process that. The operator who made the Gate 2 call — "I'm pulling us out" — needs to debrief it. This room exists because the hardest part of watching a machine trade your capital is the watching.
THE LOUNGE
No screens. Zero. Comfortable seating. Physical trade journals (the act of writing by hand is the decompression). Coffee station. Natural light. Quiet. Post-shift operators come here first before they leave the building.
WELLNESS ROOM
Prayer/meditation space. Non-denominational. Available 24/7. Sometimes the only thing an operator needs at 3AM on skeleton shift is five minutes of silence that isn't the hum of servers.
TEAM KITCHEN
Full kitchen. Shared meals build the culture that shared screens cannot. Shift A and Shift B overlap here during handover lunch. The 13:00–14:00 handover window is also a meal.
FITNESS CORNER
Compact but equipped. Sitting for 8 hours watching charts destroys bodies. Basic weights, stretch area, standing desks are standard on the trading hall — but this is the dedicated space.
GARDEN / TERRACE
Outdoor space. Even a small one. Natural air, natural light, a view that isn't a candlestick chart. Designed so operators can step outside without leaving the security perimeter.
BASEMENT
INFRASTRUCTURE & CONTINUITY
The part nobody sees that keeps everything alive
PRIMARY SERVER ROOM
Rack-mounted VPS infrastructure for all 20 desks. Climate controlled (18°C constant). Redundant networking — dual ISP with automatic failover. Biometric + keycard access. No windows.
POWER & BATTERY ROOM
Lithium-grade battery bank (data-centre specification) — minimum 72-hour autonomous capacity at full building load, no exceptions. Automatic switchover < 10ms from solar feed. No diesel, no PHCN dependency. The building's primary power is the campus solar farm; the battery bank is the backup. Even in a worst-case total solar failure, 72 hours is enough to fly replacement components from anywhere on earth. It will never come to that — but the guarantee exists.
NETWORK OPERATIONS
Dedicated fibre links to primary brokers. Latency monitoring. VPN tunnels to cloud trading floor (Stage 05 redundancy). If the building loses connectivity, the cloud instances continue trading independently.
SOLAR & ENERGY MANAGEMENT
Central inverter array and energy management system. Monitors campus-wide solar generation (ground farm + building rooftop + wall cladding). Load balancing between generation, battery storage, and live consumption. Real-time dashboard on the Bridge. Designed to Tier IV data centre standards — fault-tolerant, continuously available, no single point of failure in the power chain.
BACKUP & DR
Offsite backup to Komir's cloud infrastructure (encrypted, daily). Disaster recovery plan: if the building is unusable, all desks fail over to cloud instances within 15 minutes. The building is the preferred mode, not the only mode.

DEPLOYMENT SEQUENCE — AGENTS FIRST, HUMANS WHEN SCALE DEMANDS
PHASE 1 — NOW
Solo Operator + EA
Kenneth runs the engine personally. One account, one chart set, full manual oversight. The canary. This is where the edge is proven and the EA earns trust. No building, no team — just the engine and the founder.
PHASE 2 — CLOUD FLOOR
Agentic Operation
Multiple accounts on cloud VPS. The EA runs 24/7 without a human in the loop. Monitoring is automated — push notifications on gate fires, daily P&L summaries, anomaly alerts. An AI agent handles the shift handover that a human would do later. This is the virtual trading floor — the building's operations running as software before they run as rooms.
PHASE 3 — FIRST DESKS
First Human Operators
Capital under management crosses the threshold where a human in the loop reduces risk better than automation alone. First 3–5 desks activated. Shared office space, not a purpose-built facility yet. Performance Psychologist engaged on retainer. The culture is built here — before the building.
PHASE 4 — THE BUILDING
Purpose-Built Facility
20 desks. 60+ operators. Full infrastructure. The building in this document. This happens when capital under management, client count, and regulatory requirements demand a permanent, secure, institutional-grade facility. By this point, every system, every protocol, every handover process has been battle-tested in Phases 1–3.
DESIGN STANDARDS — BUILT FOR A DECADE
SECURITY
Biometric access on every internal door. CCTV with 90-day retention on all common areas (never inside the Psychology Suite). No personal devices past reception. Visitor escort policy — no exceptions. Perimeter security with 24/7 guard post. All servers in a locked, climate-controlled room with independent access logs.
CONNECTIVITY
Dual ISP with automatic failover (< 50ms switchover). Dedicated fibre to primary brokers. Internal network segmented — trading VLAN isolated from corporate. 4G/5G backup on a third carrier. Latency monitoring with alerts at > 100ms. The EA's connection to the broker is the single most critical wire in the building.
POWER — FULLY SOLAR
Campus solar farm as primary generation — a dedicated section of the campus grounds, plus all rooftop and wall-mounted panels and solar cladding on every viable surface. Separate solar array dedicated to the Capital Building alone. Lithium-grade battery bank (data-centre spec, same cooling and management architecture used in Tier III/IV facilities) providing minimum 72-hour autonomy at full load. No diesel generators. No national grid dependency. PHCN does not enter the building. Monthly battery discharge tests under full load. The building generates and stores its own power — permanently, independently, unconditionally.
HUMAN FACTORS
Natural light on the trading hall and human wing (floor-to-ceiling on at least two walls). Acoustic treatment between zones. Ergonomic seating rated for 8-hour use. Standing desk option at every station. Air quality monitoring (CO₂ levels affect decision-making). Temperature 22–24°C constant on the trading floor.
SCALABILITY
Modular desk infrastructure — power, data, and display connections pre-wired to 30 positions (10 reserve beyond 20 active). Cable management designed for desk addition without floor disruption. Server room sized for 2× current rack requirements. The building should not need structural modification to grow from 20 to 30 desks.
CONTINUITY
The building is the preferred mode, not the only mode. Every desk has a cloud mirror. If the building is evacuated, all desks fail over to cloud VPS within 15 minutes. Annual DR drill: full building evacuation, cloud failover, and recovery. The EA does not know or care where it runs.
